Proposed USB serial interface

set_wheel_speeds(left_deg_s, right_deg_s) streams targets for navigation. move_wheels(left_deg, right_deg, max_deg_s) starts one paired relative move for a nudge or calibration.

Turn geometry

Equal and opposite wheel motion pivots about the drive axle midpoint. The Pi plans the maneuver and checks clearance for the full chassis, including the front overhang.

Firmware status

These USB motion commands are proposed for the ESP32-S3-Zero. The current drive-controller firmware serves a Wi-Fi interface.
